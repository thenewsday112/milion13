3 Recipes You Need to Bring to Thanksgiving

📅 Nov 24, 2025  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

Thanksgiving is just a few days away and I know that your kitchen might get pretty chaotic with lots of chefs in the kitchen. So, I wanted to give you 3 easy, healthy, and delicious recipes that you could make and impress your in laws, your family, and friends! 

From Mac n Cheese to the perfect holiday cocktail...

BAKED MAC ‘N’ CHEESE

SERVES: 4

INGREDIENTS
• 1 tbsp avocado oil
• 1 onion, chopped
• 2 garlic cloves, chopped
• Sprinkle of salt and pepper
• 1 tbsp hempseeds
• 1-8 oz. box chickpea pasta (serves 4)
• 3 cups whole milk

• 2 tbsp cassava flour
• 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
• 1⁄2 cup raw Gruyere cheese
• 1⁄2 cup raw sharp cheddar
• 1⁄2 cup raw sheep’s milk
• 1⁄2 cup raw Manchego
• 2 cups cooked broccoli

DIRECTIONS
1. Add olive oil to a pan over medium heat and begin to sauté the onion and garlic until cooked through and slightly browned. Add a pinch of salt and pepper.
2. In a medium saucepan, bring water to boil and cook chickpea pasta according to directions on the box.
3. **Pre-heat oven to 400 F while pasta finishes cooking.
4. Once pasta is finished, strain it and use the saucepan to begin making the cheese mixture.
5. Over medium heat, add mustard and milk, and slowly begin to add in the coconut flour, whisking regularly.
6. Slowly drop in the cheese, adding a 1⁄2 cup at a time while continuing to stir.

7. Once all cheese is melted, add in the onion and garlic and mix well.
8. Stir in the broccoli and pasta until everything is coated evenly.
9. Finally, coat a ceramic or oven-safe dish with non-stick spray, and place the mac ‘n’ cheese in the dish.
10. Top with hemp seeds and place in the oven for about 15 minutes.
11. Remove from the oven when the top is lightly browned, plate it, and enjoy!

SAVORY OVEN ROASTED CARROTS

SERVES: 4-6

INGREDIENTS
Carrots
• 2-3 large bunches carrots
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 1⁄3 cup raw pumpkin seeds
• 1⁄4 cup pomegranate seeds
• Freshly ground sea salt, to taste
• Freshly ground black pepper,
to taste
• Fresh parsley to garnish

Zesty Yogurt Sauce
• 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
• 2 tsp fresh garlic, minced
• 1⁄4 cup tahini
• 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
• 1⁄2 tsp ground cumin
• Freshly ground sea salt, to taste

DIRECTIONS
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Wash, pat dry, peel, and cut the stems off the carrots.
3. On a large baking sheet, add the carrots and drizzle with olive oil, then sprinkle with salt and pepper, and place in the oven. Roast for about 20 minutes.

4. In a small blender or food processor, add all the ingredients for the yogurt sauce and
blend until smooth. You can slowly add a splash of water to help thin the mixture until you get to a smooth consistency.
5. When you have about 5 minutes left on the carrots, add the pumpkin seeds to a small baking sheet and allow them to roast for a few minutes.
6. When carrots are easy to pierce with a fork and look nice and roasted, remove from the oven. Add them to a dish. When you’re ready to serve, drizzle them with the yogurt sauce and sprinkle the pumpkin and pomegranate seeds all over. Garnish with parsley and add salt and pepper to taste.

APPLE CIDER THYME BEE'S KNEES

SERVES: 1

INGREDIENTS
• 4 fresh thyme sprigs
• 2 tbsp raw honey (you can sub with monk
fruit to make og sugar)
• 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
• 2-3 lemons, juiced
• 1⁄2 cup water
• 2 oz gin (or vodka)
• Extra thyme and star anise to garnish
(optional)

DIRECTIONS
1. Add thyme, ACV, honey, and water in a pan over medium-high heat. Bring to a boil, then remove it from the heat. Stir regularly.
2. Let it cool, then strain the mixture.
3. Fill a shaker with ice and add the lemon juice, ACV mixture, and alcohol.
4. Shake her up and serve in a cute coupe glass with a sprig of thyme and star anise to garnish.

The Perfect Palm Springs Hideaway 🌴

📅 Oct 29, 2025  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

A peaceful spot to reset, reconnect, and dream big for the year ahead

There’s something so special about escaping to Palm Springs for a few quiet days in the sun; especially when it’s time to dream, plan, and set intentions for a brand-new year. Ryan and I recently snuck away to The Lucille Palm Springs for our 2026 planning retreat, and it was truly the perfect place to rest, reset, and get inspired.

This was our second time staying at The Lucille, and it’s safe to say it’s become a yearly tradition for us. There’s just something about this spot... The calm energy, the attention to detail, the genuinely kind staff that makes it feel like a home away from home.

Nestled in the heart of downtown, The Lucille is a true boutique gem. It’s quiet, peaceful, and beautifully designed, with mid-century charm and thoughtful touches that make every corner feel curated just for you. From the serene pool deck to the cozy, sun-drenched rooms, everything about it invites you to slow down and breathe.

Why We Love Staying Here

We came to Palm Springs to do our 2026 yearly planning. We always love to take a few days to dream big, set goals, and create space for new ideas and The Lucille was the perfect backdrop. We started each morning with coffee by the pool, mapped out our plans in the sunshine, and ended our evenings with long walks and cozy dinners.

The best part? It’s incredibly walkable. You can leave your car behind and explore some of our favorite nearby spots:

  • 🗿 Forever Marilyn Statue: Just a few minutes away, this iconic Palm Springs landmark is a must-see (and such a fun photo op!).

  • 🖼️ Palm Springs Art Museum: Perfect for an inspiring midday break. It’s filled with incredible exhibits and just steps from the hotel.

  • ☕️ Koffi North Palm Springs: My go-to for a quick coffee or smoothie before heading out for the day.

  • 🥗 Farm: The cutest French-inspired café tucked in downtown. Their brunch is everything — think fresh ingredients, cozy garden vibes, and great service.

The Lucille Experience

Every detail at The Lucille feels personal. From the warm welcome when you arrive to the way the staff goes above and beyond to make your stay special. They truly care, and it shows. It’s rare to find a place that’s both design-forward and deeply personal, but The Lucille has mastered that balance.

It’s the kind of spot where you can spend the whole afternoon lounging by the pool, journaling under the palms, or sneaking in a massage before dinner and still feel like you’re right in the heart of the action when you’re ready to explore.

We left feeling rested, inspired, and grateful, with our 2026 plans mapped out and our hearts full. It’s a trip we look forward to every year now, and I couldn’t recommend it more if you’re craving a little sunshine, stillness, and inspiration.

Thank you again to The Lucille Palm Springs for hosting us so beautifully. If you’re planning your next getaway, this is one you’ll want to bookmark. ☀️💛
